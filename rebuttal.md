Response to Reviewer jttg20 (**R3**):

**R3-Q1 Difference from Other Methods:** While VDMini shares some high-level concepts with existing trial-and-error approaches, it introduces several key innovations specifically tailored for video generation. First, our pruning strategy is guided not only by quantitative FVD metrics, which objectively measure the fidelity and diversity of generated videos, but also by empirical user studies regarding the motion details, allowing us to identify and preserve subtle temporal dynamics that may not be fully captured by automated metrics alone. Second, we propose a novel distillation framework that leverages both real-world samples and synthetic data generated by the teacher model.

**R3-Q2 Role of different layers:** Since the low-resolution layers have significant impact on the reconstruction of texture, which results in increase of FVD score. Additionally, we have provided the detailed analysis in the supplementary material (i.e., Line 156-158).

**R3-Q3 Details about importance evaluation:** As introduced in Section 3.2, the importance of a block is evaluated by removing it and comparing the FVD score. Since some blocks have different input and output tensor shapes, we use a Convolution layer named ``conv_shortcut`` in the pre-trained U-Net model to replace the entire block.
